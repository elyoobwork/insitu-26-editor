Text

Each block is a group of dot letters with its own size and place on the artboard (positions are on a 1080 × 1350 board). The built-in In Situ Dots font is used.

Letters are drawn on a grid of dots. Dot grid sets the dot spacing, text size sets the letter height in dots. Pixel fonts give the cleanest result; the Korean fonts handle Hangul. These settings are shared by all three tabs.

Motion

The dots always start scattered. Click the artboard (or Gather) to pull them into letters, click again to scatter. Recording restarts from scattered.

Mouse

On the videos (and the Lens effect) the pointer bends the letters. Click to pin the bend in place; pin several, click a pinned one to remove it. Pins are kept in recordings, PNG and SVG.
